How we navigate it

We start with your needs, not a vendor’s pitch.

Technology decisions often go wrong before any code is written: chosen for the hype, the demo, or because it is what the last team knew. We work the other way round. First we understand what you need. Then we narrow the field against the criteria that matter to your business, and recommend the option that fits — even when that is the unglamorous one, or something you already own.
